## Audience
University students, researchers, graduate scholars, and engineers seeking high-density, multi-hour study and scientific synthesis of complex literature without distractions or hallucinated summaries.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
An IDE-inspired, high-density scientific research studio that treats research literature with the computational precision of source code. It replaces consumer AI chatboxes with a disciplined, multi-pane workbench featuring persistent document trees, split-screen reading and AI interrogation, live provenance margins, and a keyboard-first command palette (`Cmd+K`).

### OWN-WORLD
Deep obsidian research slate (`#090D16`) and elevated workbench panes (`#0F172A`), precise hairline dividers (`#1E293B`), monospace telemetry badges, electric cyan telemetry (`#0EA5E9`), emerald provenance anchors (`#10B981`), and high-density tabular typography.

### STORY
The visitor enters an active research IDE loaded with *Vaswani et al. (2017) Attention Is All You Need*. The left pane anchors document hierarchies and ingestion queues; the center displays the primary academic document with highlighted attention spans and interactive marginalia; the right pane hosts an interactive AI Socratic tutor, active recall deck, and attention weight matrix. Visitors can trigger the `Cmd+K` command palette or launch the workspace instantly with a 1-click Scholar Pass.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
A high-density studio status bar with document telemetry and `Cmd+K` command palette trigger, leading directly into a 3-pane research workbench: collapsible document tree on the left, primary monograph with line-number anchors in the center, and interactive AI interrogation scratchpad on the right.
